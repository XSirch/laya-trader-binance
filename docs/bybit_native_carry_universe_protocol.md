# Bybit native carry universe: fixed public-quote screen

Specified after the December 2026 BTC/ETH quote screen, before reading the broader universe's current books. This is an exploratory, post-observation extension; a positive row is not a fill, historical return or robust-profit proof. No orders are submitted.

- Query official spread instrument metadata for all available BTC, ETH and SOL combinations, following pagination. Record every `CarryTrade` combination with status `Trading` and future delivery time. For every such combination, read its public 25-level native spread book once. Read one corresponding ordinary spot book for each base coin. Preserve the API payloads, timing and errors. Report every eligible combination without selecting a winner after seeing quotes.
- For each combination, use both fixed intended spot notionals, 500 and 1,000 USDT. Round quantities down by the live spread `lotSize`, reject a quantity outside `minSize`/`maxSize`, and require its entire quantity to fit in displayed spread bid levels. Compute the sell-combo bid VWAP and the same conditional hold-to-expiry net cash and reserved-capital return in [the earlier fixed economics protocol](bybit_native_spread_entry_economics_protocol.md). Use the identical fee, settlement, exit, spot-index and 1% annual capital-charge assumptions.
- In addition to the earlier costs, subtract 0.25% of spot notional as a further adverse execution/index-basis stress. Show both base and stressed results. A preliminary candidate for longer forward observation must have a positive two-sided spot and spread book, spread and spot `ts - cts` in [0, 10] seconds, their `ts` values within five seconds, full displayed bid quantity for **both** intended sizes, at least 30 days to delivery, and at least 4% annualized stressed net return on reserved capital at **both** sizes. This threshold is a research gate, not a promised account yield.
- Even a candidate requires repeated forward quotes, actual account fee and collateral data, and completed after-cost outcomes. A failed freshness criterion is insufficient evidence of current liquidity; a resting quote could remain executable. No public book snapshot proves an order would fill.

Primary sources: [Bybit spread instruments](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/instrument), [spread books](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/orderbook), [ordinary spot books](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/market/orderbook), [spread trading FAQ](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Spread-Trading).
