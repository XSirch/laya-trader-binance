# Bybit native carry spread: execution feasibility

Reviewed 2026-09-26 UTC. This is a source and live-market feasibility check for the BTC/ETH spot-versus-dated-future research. It is not an executable profit claim, a historical return estimate, or an instruction to place an order. The live values below are transient snapshots from public Bybit API endpoints.

## Mechanism and relevance

Bybit's native **Spread Trading** supports a `CarryTrade` combination of USDT spot and a USDT dated future. Selling the combo buys spot and sells the dated future. Bybit says each fill executes both legs in matching quantities or neither leg, although a larger combo order may be partially filled. This addresses independent-leg fill risk for the quantity actually matched. It does not guarantee any fill, closing liquidity, future profitability, or protection from normal margin and liquidation rules. [Bybit Spread Trading FAQ](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Spread-Trading), [introduction and order direction](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/Introduction-to-Spread-Trading).

The spread has its **own order book**; its liquidity is separate from the ordinary spot and futures books. Consequently, prior ordinary-leg trade-volume or order-book checks cannot establish native-combo liquidity, and zero ordinary futures trades in a given five-minute interval do not prove that the spread book was empty then. This is an inference from the documented separate matching venues, not an observation of a historical spread book. [Bybit Spread Trading FAQ](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Spread-Trading), [spread book API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/orderbook).

## Cost and account constraints

Bybit states native spread fees are 50% below placing the two legs separately, with the discount applied to the user's VIP rates. Its published VIP 0 schedule lists spot taker 0.1000% and futures taker 0.0550%, but Bybit says actual rates can vary by region and directs users to their own fee-rate page. As a conditional arithmetic illustration, halving those rates produces 0.0500% spot plus 0.0275% futures per combo execution; opening and closing near the same notional would cost roughly **0.155% of one leg's notional in trading fees**, before spread crossing, financing, settlement, margin funding, and changing prices. This is **not** a verified account fee or net return. [Spread fee rule](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Spread-Trading), [Bybit fee schedule and regional caveat](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/Trading-Fee-Structure).

The combination requires an eligible derivatives region and supports Cross Margin or Portfolio Margin, not Isolated Margin; its positions remain subject to normal margin and liquidation treatment. The spot leg may be unleveraged if margin trading is disabled. Once a dated future expires, the spot leg does not close automatically. These conditions must be checked on the user's actual account before treating the route as executable. [Eligibility, margin mode, liquidation and expiry FAQ](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Spread-Trading), [spread introduction](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/Introduction-to-Spread-Trading).

The [create-order API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/trade/create-order) supports `Limit`/`Market` and `IOC`/`FOK`/`GTC`/`PostOnly`; a successful create response is asynchronous acknowledgement, not fill proof. A future execution study would need confirmed order status and leg fills, including fees, rather than assuming the quoted size traded. No order was submitted in this review.

## Historical-evidence boundary and next gate

Bybit's [API changelog](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/changelog/v5) marks Spread Trading API introduction on 2025-04-14. The public spread [order-book API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/orderbook) is a current snapshot with no historical timestamp parameter, and [recent public spread trades](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/recent-trade) accept a symbol and at most 1,000 recent records, with no date-range parameter. I found no verified first-party downloadable historical **spread** book archive. This does not prove none exists through another Bybit channel. It means this review cannot reconstruct the exact July 1, 2026 native spread bid/ask, or retroactively use the native facility to validate the 2022-2024 ordinary-leg replay. The ordinary [public spot and futures trade archives](https://public.bybit.com/) are different matching venues.

For the research's **robust after-cost** criterion, the actionable path is a read-only forward observation protocol: continuously record the selected native combo's full 25-level bid/ask, `ts`, `cts`, quote sizes, relevant spot/futures marks, and public spread trades; reject stale or one-sided books; quantize the intended BTC/ETH size to the live instrument `lotSize`/`minSize`; and calculate an entry at the sell-combo bid plus a conservative closing buy-combo ask and account-specific fees, collateral use, and financing. Freeze selection and evaluation windows before examining resulting P&L. A fill would still require account-side order evidence, and any live trade requires a separate authorization. [Book API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/orderbook), [instrument API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/instrument), [ticker API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/tickers), [trade API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/spread/market/recent-trade), [fee caveat](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/Trading-Fee-Structure).

**Finding:** The native spread is a plausible way to remove independent-leg execution risk and observe a directly tradable combination. Today's API evidence does not establish persistent or fillable liquidity, historical performance, account eligibility, or robust profit after all costs.
