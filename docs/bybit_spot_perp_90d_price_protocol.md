# Bybit spot-perpetual 90-day price and funding screen

Specified after inspecting the nine funding-only outcomes, but before reading the corresponding historical spot/perpetual prices. This is a post-observation price-screen extension, not an untouched holdout, native-spread fill, realized account result or trading instruction. It evaluates whether spot/perpetual basis changes could offset the already observed funding-only deficit.

- Use BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT and SOLUSDT on Bybit, with the same three adjacent 30-day UTC windows fixed in [the funding-only protocol](bybit_native_funding_only_protocol.md): June 27-July 27, July 27-August 26, and August 26-September 25, 2026. For each of the four unique UTC boundaries, request exactly the five one-minute Bybit `spot` and `linear` candles starting 00:00, 00:01, 00:02, 00:03 and 00:04. Preserve every raw first-party API response and error. Any missing candle, nonpositive volume or malformed price makes all dependent periods unassessable; do not move the entry or exit time.
- Model buying spot and selling the matching USDT perpetual at entry, then reversing both at exit. Use the **highest** spot and **lowest** perpetual price in the five entry candles, and the **lowest** spot and **highest** perpetual price in the five exit candles. These separately adverse extremes are a conservative price screen, not simultaneous executable quotes. Fix the coin quantity at one initial spot-notional unit divided by the adverse spot entry price. Approximate short funding cash as that quantity times the adverse perpetual entry price times the sum of strictly interior historical settled funding rates from the existing first-party report `outputs/bybit_native_funding_only_screen.json` (SHA256 `0556180273221016b17a736bb07ae40d2e880cb0b16e993c7c4428f12dfbca7c`). Actual funding uses changing mark-price notional and may differ.
- Charge published illustrative VIP 0 **ordinary-leg** taker fees of 0.1000% on each spot entry/exit price and 0.0550% on each perpetual entry/exit price. Add 0.25% of initial spot notional as adverse execution/crossing stress and a 1% annual capital charge on `2.025` units of reserved capital per initial spot-notional unit, with actual 30 days/365. Ordinary-leg fees are used because historical native-combo books are unavailable. This is deliberately conservative, but neither the bar extremes nor fixed execution stress is a bound on real fills.
- Report spot P&L, perpetual P&L, funding, each cost and net cash for every symbol-window, plus normalized return on reserved capital. A symbol passes only a **price-and-funding research gate** if all three windows are assessable and positive after configured costs and its mean monthly reserved-capital return annualizes to at least 4%. Passing requires an independent future outcome, actual account fees/collateral, synchronized live bid/ask depth, and margin-path evidence before any robust-profit claim. A missing input is unassessable, never a zero return. Do not retune windows or costs after reading prices.
