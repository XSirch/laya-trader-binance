# Binance December 2026 delivery basis: prospective quote gate

Specified on 2026-09-26 after inspecting the older Binance delivery-basis replay and other-venue results, but before this protocol's new Binance book reads. This is a read-only, post-selection forward quote screen; even a passing quote is not an actual fill, a completed holding-period result or an independent validation of the older strategy.

- Fix BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT spot against their December 25, 2026 USDT-delivery futures. Obtain current symbol status, delivery time, quantity step/minimum and spot rules from official exchange metadata. Query one public 100-level spot ask book and one 100-level USD-M future bid book for each coin, plus Binance server time. Retain raw books, metadata, response times, source URLs and errors. Do not replace a failed read or change contract, coin or size.
- Evaluate **both** intended spot notionals, 500 and 1,000 USDT, for **both** coins. Round each quantity down to a common valid spot/future lot step, require the minimums and the whole quantity to fit inside the displayed spot asks and future bids, and calculate both marketable volume-weighted prices. Require the two local book receive times for a coin to differ by at most five seconds; future matching timestamp `T` must be no more than ten seconds behind the returned futures server clock. Spot REST depth has no matching-engine timestamp, so this only checks receipt alignment, not guaranteed book persistence or atomic execution.
- Model buying spot at its displayed ask VWAP, shorting the delivery future at its displayed bid VWAP, and holding until future cash settlement. Use these **illustrative conservative** cost rates, fixed before the book reads: 0.1000% spot taker at entry, 0.0500% USD-M future taker at entry, 0.0500% future settlement fee stress, 0.1000% spot taker on the later spot sale, 0.1000% spot-sale slippage, 0.1000% adverse future-settlement-index versus realizable-spot mismatch, and an **additional** 0.2500% of entry spot notional as execution/basis uncertainty stress. Charge 1.00% annual on 2.025 times entry spot notional as illustrative reserved capital through delivery. Actual account fees, collateral and exit behavior are unverified; the fixed mismatch amounts are stresses, not bounds.
- A quote is only a candidate for longer forward paper observation if **both** intended sizes for **both** coins have fully displayed entry depth, timing checks, positive conditional net cash, and at least 4% annualized conditional net return on the illustrative reserved capital **after** all configured stresses. Otherwise report the specific failing leg/size and do not initiate a shadow position. A candidate would still need account-specific fees, hedged-fill evidence, a daily margin path and completed future outcome before any robust-profit claim. No orders are authorized by this protocol.
